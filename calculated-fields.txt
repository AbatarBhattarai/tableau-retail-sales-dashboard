Tableau calculated fields — Retail Sales Dashboard
Data: retail_orders_clean.csv (1,200 orders, Jan-Dec 2024)
Field names are the CSV column names.

Total Revenue
  SUM([revenue])
  Check: $118,568.75 with no filter on.

Total Orders
  COUNT([order_id])
  Check: 1,200.

Average Order Value
  [Total Revenue] / [Total Orders]
  Check: $98.81.

Month
  DATETRUNC('month', [order_date])
  Use on Columns for the monthly trend (continuous month).

MoM Revenue Change %  (table calculation, monthly sheet only)
  (SUM([revenue]) - LOOKUP(SUM([revenue]), -1)) / ABS(LOOKUP(SUM([revenue]), -1))
  Format as %. Check: Nov 2024 = +73.2%.

Category Share %  (table calculation, category sheet only)
  SUM([revenue]) / TOTAL(SUM([revenue]))
  Format as %. Check: Clothing = 31.2%.
